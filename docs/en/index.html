01

Five panels. One question: do the numbers add up?

What the wall gives, what the system takes, what the battery gets — three lines that must reconcile. When they don't, something is drawing power behind your back.

Power flow

One strip from wall to battery. Thickness follows wattage, and it splits into system and battery while charging.

  • Three nodes: wall, system, battery
  • Thickness uses a 0.7 power curve, so low wattages still read
  • Arrows at the ends show direction

System

CPU, GPU and memory load, plus a process list ranked by energy.

  • Live curves for CPU, GPU and memory
  • Process ranking reads rusage_info_v6 — no root
  • Energy shown in watts, by differencing

History

How many Wh went in last time, how much each day this week, one CSV export.

  • SQLite on disk, survives midnight
  • The last session is kept after the cable is pulled
  • A failed export says why — it never fakes success

Charger

Negotiated volts and amps, which PD tiers it offers, and why it isn't running full.

  • Vendor and model (not every charger reports them)
  • The PD table marks the tier in use
  • When it underperforms you get a reason: trickle, thermal, or cable

Settings

Sample interval, history retention, alert thresholds, menu bar content and language.

  • Menu bar can show icon, percentage and wattage
  • Four alert types: low, full, hot, slow

The software, drawn as a machine

A shunt that turns current into a voltage, a needle dial that reads it, a paper recorder that draws the curve, a ratchet that steps once a second, a bell that rings when something is wrong. Every module maps to a part. You can drag the load on the drawing.

03

Everything it needs. Nothing it doesn't.

No account, no cloud sync, no recommendations. The data stays on this Mac.

Once a second

Sampling, menu bar and widget share one clock. Plugging in a charger shows up immediately, not on the next tick.

1 s·sampling clock

Charger check-up

Negotiated volts and amps, the PD tier table, vendor and model. When it underperforms you get a conclusion, not a raw code.

5V · 3A9V · 3A12V · 3A15V · 3A20V · 3A

Battery health

Cycle count, real full capacity, design capacity, current temperature.

92.9%·design capacity

30-day history

SQLite with WAL, one row every 2 s, log merged on launch.

Desktop widget

Current wattage and charge level without opening the panel.

04

Where the numbers come from

No estimates. Everything is read from the system's own registers.

ModuleHow it is read
Power & battery
IOKit AppleSmartBattery: instantaneous power, volts and amps, cycle count, temperature
Charger
AdapterDetails and UsbHvcMenu: negotiated tier, PD capability list, vendor and model
Per-process energy
ri_energy_nj from rusage_info_v6, differenced — no root needed
History
SQLite with WAL, one row every 2 s, log merged on launch
Rendering
Pure AppKit. The glass popover and the flow diagram are both draw(_:)
Widget
WidgetKit, reading a snapshot the app writes into an App Group
